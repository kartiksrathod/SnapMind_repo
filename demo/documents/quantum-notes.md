# Quantum Notes

Quantum computing uses qubits as its basic unit of quantum information. Unlike a classical bit, a qubit can represent a combination of 0 and 1 until it is measured.

Quantum gates transform qubits. A circuit applies gates in sequence, and measurement converts the final quantum state into classical results.

The practical value of this approach is problem-dependent. Quantum algorithms are designed for specific tasks, so a quantum computer is not a universal replacement for a classical computer.
